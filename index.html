About

I am currently a BK21 Postdoctoral Fellow at Seoul National University. Previously, I was a Simons Postdoctoral Research Scientist in the Mathematics Department of Columbia University under the supervision of Victor Kac . I received my PhD from MIT in 2017.

My research lies at the interface of mathematical physics, algebra, and integrable systems. I am particularly interested in Lax operators, W-algebras, and quantization of integrable structures.

An interview with the Simons Society of Fellows discussing my research and the connections between mathematics and creativity can be found here .

Research

Integrable Systems and Lax Operators

My research concerns integrable systems of partial differential and differential-difference equations, with an emphasis on algebraic structures behind Lax representations.

W-algebras and Hamiltonian Structures

I study classical W-algebras, Poisson vertex algebras, and their realizations through integrable hierarchies.

Quantization of Integrable Lattices

I am interested in quantization problems for discrete integrable systems and lattice analogues of classical structures.
