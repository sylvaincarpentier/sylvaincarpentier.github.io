About

I am currently a BK21 Postdoctoral Fellow at Seoul National University. Previously, I was a Simons Postdoctoral Research Scientist in the Mathematics Department of Columbia University under the supervision of Victor Kac . I received my PhD from MIT in 2017.

My research lies at the interface of mathematical physics, algebra, and integrable systems. More specifically, I work on Lax operators, W-algebras, Hamiltonian structures, and quantization of integrable systems.
